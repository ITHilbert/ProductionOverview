1.0.0 (2026-11-01)
- First release.
- List of all production buildings with productivity, main reason for downtime and current state.
- Time range: current month, last month, last 3 or 12 months.
- Filters: name/type, efficiency threshold, hide paused, hide "output full", hide auxiliary buildings.
- "By type" tab with average per building type; checkbox per type to exclude it (e.g. cooling towers).
- Hover highlights the building on the map, click flies there and opens its inspector.
- English and German.
